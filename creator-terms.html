10. Commissions and payouts

You earn commissions at the rate and on the terms shown on the creator page and your creator dashboard at the time a sale is made. Payouts are sent to the payout account you give us on the schedule shown there.

"Revenue" means the amount Apple (or another app store) actually pays Meal Pic for a subscription or purchase made by a person who used your link or code, after the store's own commission (currently 15% to 30%), and after any refunds, chargebacks, reversals, and sales or similar taxes. It does not include free trials until they convert to a paid subscription. A subscription renewal counts as a new sale, so you keep earning on every renewal for as long as that subscriber stays and your account is in good standing. If a payment is refunded or reversed after you have been paid on it, we may deduct that amount from your next payout.

Founding rate. Where we offer a higher percentage to a limited number of creators (for example, the first five to join), that rate applies only to creators who complete sign-up while the offer is open, as shown on the creator page at the time, and only to subscribers they refer. We may change the rate for new creators at any time; a change does not reduce the rate on subscribers you already referred.
