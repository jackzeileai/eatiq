Questions

Anything else? Email help@mealpic.app.

How and when do I get paid?

Every Friday, by Venmo, PayPal, Zelle or Cash App, whichever you pick when you sign up. Once a subscriber's payment comes in, your half goes out that Friday.

What counts as a sale?

Any real payment from someone who came through your link or used your code. Free trials count once they turn into a payment. Refunds don't pay out.

Do my followers get a discount?

No. Your code makes sure you get credit. Your followers pay the normal price.

How many followers do I need?

None. Anyone can join. It's free and there's no contract.

What should I post?

You don't have to guess. When you join you get our library of proven reels, with why each one worked, and a step-by-step playbook for making your own. The short version: film yourself actually using it, snap a meal, show the calories pop up, and end on your code.

Where can I see my numbers?

Your creator dashboard shows downloads, trials, subscribers, recurring revenue, your next payout and past payouts, live.
