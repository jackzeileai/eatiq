FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is it?

Every number is an estimate. Eat IQ measures portions from the photo, prices each ingredient against nutrition data, and shows you the breakdown so you can correct any line. Good light and a photo that fills the frame help a lot. If a scan is way off, edit the item — your correction is remembered for next time.

What does it cost?

The app is free to download. Eat IQ Pro unlocks unlimited scanning: $29.99 a year (about $2.49 a month) or $9.99 a month, with a 3-day free trial. Prices in USD; Canada is priced in CAD.

How do I cancel my subscription or free trial?

Subscriptions go through Apple, so cancellation does too:

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone and tap your name.
  2. Tap Subscriptions.
  3. Tap Eat IQ, then Cancel Subscription.

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid being charged.

I paid but the app still shows a paywall

On the paywall screen tap Restore Purchases, and make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID you bought with. Switched phones or Apple IDs? That's usually the cause.

What happens to my photos and data?

Before your first scan the app asks for consent to send your food photo (or voice description) to our AI provider for analysis. Tapping "Not Now" sends nothing. Your meals back up to your account so a new phone gets them back. You can delete your account and all its data from inside the app. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Does it work with Apple Health?

Yes. Meals you log are written to Health, and edits and deletes are carried across — not just new entries. Workouts and active energy from Health can count toward your daily goal.

Is Eat IQ medical advice?

No. Eat IQ is a general wellness tool, not a medical device, and nothing in it is medical, nutritional or dietary advice. Please consult a doctor or a registered dietitian before making significant changes to your diet.
