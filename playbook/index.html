Meal Pic Creative Brief

What to make, what we need from you, and what good looks like.

Content

Meal Pic should show up inside a video you'd make anyway: you scan a real meal, the calories load, you keep going. It's a thing you use, not an ad read.

  • Priority 1 · Seamless integration. A what-I-eat-in-a-day, day-in-my-life or recipe video where you scan your food with Meal Pic on camera.
  • Priority 2 · Dedicated video. A video about the app itself: how you track calories now, why it stuck.

Important note

  • Send every video to us for approval before you post it. Email it to partnerships@mealpic.app.
  • Posting requirements. Say your code in the video, put your link (mealpic.app/YOURCODE) in your bio, and tag @mealpic.app.
  • Reply to at least 3 comments about Meal Pic on every post.
  • Send us the post link as soon as it's up, same email.

Examples of a good promotion

Example 1Eating on camera, scan the meal · 42.5M views Example 2950 cal vs 210 cal · 10.3M views

Why are these good promotions?

  1. They hook people who actually care about calories, so the views turn into downloads.
  2. The app shows up on a real plate in a real moment, not in a pitch.
  3. The first 3 seconds open a loop you want to see closed.

Every integration must include 3 parts

  1. The Hook · the first 3 seconds. Get the right people's interest.
  2. The Meat · show the scan and why it matters to you.
  3. The CTA · tell them what to do and give them a why.

Examples of a bad promotion

Example 1Gatorade chicken · 3.7M views

Why is this a bad promotion?

  1. It hooked the comedy audience, not the fitness audience.
  2. Big views, almost no downloads. The wrong people watched.
  3. The app was a prop in a joke, not a thing anyone wanted to use.

Video ideas

  1. What I eat in a day. Scan every meal as you go. The calories add up on screen.
  2. “I tracked everything I ate for 7 days.” Day-in-my-life with the app in every meal, and what changed.
  3. High-protein recipe. Cook it, plate it, scan it at the end to show the macros.
