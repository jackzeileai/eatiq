Make great videos with Meal Pic in them

You know your audience better than we do. No script, no approval. The one must: show the app working on a real meal.

What to aim for

Two jobs, in this order.

1. Promote the app

Make the app a big part of the video, not a half-second cutaway. Snap your food, the calories load, then edit them.

2. Get views

Make the best video you can so it reaches as many people as possible.

Views × how well you promote the app = money for you.

The rules

Four things, every post.

ApprovalNone needed. Post when it’s ready. Not sure about an idea? Text or email us, but you don’t have to.
Get downloadsSay your code in the video and put mealpic.app/YOURCODE in your bio.
Get creditTag @mealpic.app and use #mealpicapp so the video counts toward your monthly total.
Get more downloadsReply to at least 3 comments about Meal Pic. “What app is that?” → “It’s Meal Pic! Use code YOURCODE for 3 days free.”

Videos that sell

Copy what these do.

They show the magic moment

Point the camera at food and the calories pop up. People think “wait, what app is that?”

The app is the main character

The whole video revolves around what the app shows. Take it out and there’s no video.

The theme fits our people

Losing weight, getting lean, eating more protein. Those viewers download.

Every video needs 3 parts

Miss one and the views don’t turn into downloads.

1. The theme

Grab the RIGHT people. Open a question only someone into the gym, weight loss or calorie tracking would stop for.

2. The magic moment

In the first 15 seconds: snap, the macros load, keep going. One line on why it matters to you.

3. The CTA

Short, with a reason. “Link in my bio gets you 3 days free.”

Videos that don’t

Big views, almost no downloads.

Wrong crowd

It hooked comedy fans, not people who want to eat better. Same with a mukbang: they watch someone eat, they don’t count.

The app was a tangent

It showed up for half a second and didn’t fit the video.
