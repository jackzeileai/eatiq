Creator playbook

A winning video comes down to three parts.

The hook, the meat, and the CTA.

1. The hook

The hook has two goals:

  1. Get people's interest.
  2. Make sure it's targeted to the right person.

If it isn't targeted to the right person, you might get a lot of views, but nobody downloads the app and buys. It's like selling steak to vegans. You might have the best steak in the world, but a room full of vegans isn't interested.

Getting interest. The best way to make a viral hook is to open a loop. Create a curiosity gap and make it very visual. Starting in the action with text on screen is a great way to do that. Keep it simple and clean, not too many elements. The loop can be curiosity, shock, or a story.

Starts in the action, text on screen, one thing happening.

Targeting the right person. These formats work:

  • Day in my life
  • What I eat in a day
  • Workout videos
  • Storytime
  • A skit about health, fitness or the gym
  • Healthy recipes
  • High-protein recipes

2. The meat

This is where you show the value and demonstrate the app. The magic moment, the thing that really sells it, is the food scan. It's three steps:

  1. Take a picture of the food.
  2. The macros load.
  3. Edit it.

If you don't show the value of the app enough, people won't be interested and won't download.

There are lots of ways to show value. One is anti-value:

“Here's why I'm returning this thing.”

“It's not because X. It's not because Y. It's not because Z.”

X, Y and Z are all good features.

3. The CTA

The CTA tells people what to do after watching, and that's what gets the conversion. Keep it short and sweet.

Most importantly, give the viewer a reason why they should click your link. What are they getting? Usually that's:

“Click my link for a free 3-day trial.”

“Click my link for 3 days free.”

That's the “because”. Now it makes sense why they should go through your link.

That's it. Hook, meat, CTA.
