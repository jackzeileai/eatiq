Meal Pic Creative Brief

What to make, what we need from you, and what good looks like.

Content

Make great videos with Meal Pic in them. You know your audience better than we do, so you get full creative control. No script, no approval. The one must: show the app working on a real meal.

  • Priority 1 · Sell the app. Make the app a big part of the video, not a half-second cutaway. Show the magic moment: snap your food, the calories load, then edit them.
  • Priority 2 · Get views. Make the best video you can so it reaches as many people as possible.

Views × how well you sell the app = money for you.

Important note

  • No approval needed. Post when it's ready. If you're not sure about an idea, text or email us, but you don't have to.
  • Get downloads: say your code in the video and put your link (mealpic.app/YOURCODE) in your bio.
  • Get credit: tag @mealpic.app and use #mealpicapp so the video counts toward your monthly total.
  • Get even more downloads: reply to at least 3 comments about Meal Pic on every post. If someone asks "what app is that?", answer: "It's Meal Pic! Use code YOURCODE for 3 days free."

Examples of a good promotion

Example 1950 cal vs 210 cal · 10.3M views Example 2New normal Example 3What I eat in a day (high protein)

Why are these good promotions?

  1. They show the magic moment. Point the camera at food, the calories pop up. People see it and think "wait, what app is that?" That moment sells the app for you.
  2. The app is the main character. The whole video revolves around what the app shows. Take the app out and there's no video.
  3. The theme is something our people care about. Losing weight, getting lean, eating more protein, eating healthy. Those viewers download. The wrong viewers just watch.

Every integration must include 3 parts

  1. The theme. Grab the RIGHT people. Open a question they want answered, about something only people into working out, weight loss, calorie tracking or the gym would stop for.
  2. The magic moment. Snap the photo, the calories and macros load, keep going. Do this in the first 15 seconds. Say in one line why it matters to you ("I stopped guessing," "I'm finally losing weight without weighing my food").
  3. The CTA. Short, with a reason. "Link in my bio gets you 3 days free."

Examples of a bad promotion

Example 1Gatorade chicken · 3.7M views

Example 2: a mukbang. The audience is there to watch someone eat 8,000 calories, not to count their own.

Why is this a bad promotion?

  1. Wrong crowd. It hooked comedy fans, not people who want to eat better. Big views, almost no downloads.
  2. The app was a tangent. It showed up for half a second and didn't fit the video.

Video ideas

  1. What I eat in a day. Scan every meal as you go, and the total adds up at the end.
  2. “I tracked everything I ate for 7 days.” A day-in-my-life with Meal Pic at every meal, and what changed.
  3. Guess the calories. Two meals that look the same (or a "healthy" one that isn't). Guess first, then scan to reveal.
  4. High-protein recipe. Cook it, plate it, scan it at the end to show the macros.
