A successful video has three parts.

Nail all three and the video gets views and sales.

The first 3 seconds. Get the right people's interest.

Show the app and why it's valuable.

1. The Hook

The first 3 seconds of the video.

What is a hook?

  • The algorithm grades it by skip rate: the % of people who leave in the first 3 seconds. Lower is better.
  • It's one of the biggest factors in whether a video goes viral.

A hook has two jobs

1. Get people's interest
2. Get the right people's interest

Job 1: Get people's interest

How? You open a loop.

What is a loop? A question the viewer wants answered. It can be stated or implied, verbal or non-verbal.

Two types of loops
A. VisualSomething interesting on screen. Best for grabbing attention.
B. VerbalSomething you say or put in text. Best for grabbing the right attention and keeping people watching.
Small things that help
Don't reinvent the wheelCopy winning hooks.
Start in the actionPut context in text on screen, don't explain it.

Job 2: Get the right people's interest

The hook and the concept of the video need to be aimed at people who'd actually download a calorie tracking app. If not, you get views but no sales.

Concepts that work
Fitness vlogs
Day in my life
What I eat in a day
Healthy cooking recipes
High protein recipes
Fitness videos in general
Talking head story time
Anti-example

The Gatorade chicken video got a ton of views, but it hooked the comedy audience, not the fitness audience. Big views, small sales.

2. The Meat

Where you show the app and why it's valuable.

What do you show?

The magic moment: the scan feature.

1. Take a picture of your food
2. Results load
3. Edit if needed

Why does it matter to you?

Pick one or two.

Helped you lose weight
Makes calorie tracking easy
You're finally consistent and feel better

How do you frame it?

A. Friend recommendation“Here's a useful thing I use.”
B. Reverse value“Here's why I'm canceling my Meal Pic subscription.” It's not because: valuable feature 1, valuable feature 2, valuable feature 3.

3. The CTA

What you tell the viewer to do at the end.

How do you do it?

Keep it short. Give them a why.

“Click the link in my bio to get 3 days free.”
