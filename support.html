Eat IQ

Calorie and macro tracking, by photo · Church Street Studios

Point your camera at a meal and Eat IQ estimates its calories, protein, carbohydrates and fat — breaking a composed plate into its parts so you can correct any one of them. It also reads barcodes and nutrition labels, searches a food database, and syncs what you log to Apple Health.
