How To Make Viral Videos

  • Before I teach you how to make a viral video it is important to understand how the algorithm works.
  • Lucky for us Instagram tells us exactly what it’s looking for, in order of importance.
  • The biggest 3 factors are skip rate, share rate, and like rate. I will teach you how to master all of them with my viral video formula.

1. Hook

2. Reason to keep watching

3. Shareable moment

1. The Hook

  • The hook is the opening of the video.
  • It is whatever gets the attention of your audience.
  • It’s THE biggest factor on if a video goes viral.
  • The algorithm grades it by skip rate: the % of people who leave in the first 3 seconds. Lower is better.
  • On IG & TikTok there are no titles or thumbnails. Instead, the opening frames of your video are what is setting the viewer’s expectations (and “hooking” them).
  • Viewers decide if they are going to watch the video within the first few seconds. If your opening doesn’t hook them it’s game over.
  • Every video that has ever gone viral ever has a good hook.
  • There are an infinite number of good hooks, but I’ll teach you how I like to think about them.
  • A good hook gets their attention but then also promises that the rest of the video will have value (ie the rest of the video will be entertaining or teach them a good piece of knowledge etc).

Visual

  • This is often what carries a video right now.
  • You want anything that is intriguing:
    • A hot girl dancing
    • A rat in a living room
    • A guy hanging off a sign
    • A really yummy looking chocolate cake
    • A kid running in a horse walker
    • 2 guys blindfolded playing a game
    • Any cool piece of action. Sometimes I start videos with the most interesting part, which is the ending.
    • A cute dog
    • A chicken eating a snake
Pro tips
  • Steal other people’s hooks
  • Start in the action
  • Keep it visually simple (reduce elements)

Verbal

  • This (in my experience) is a little less important than the visual hook for getting views.
  • But it is more important for getting THE RIGHT people to watch your video (more on that later).
  • These videos are not super visually intriguing but have crazy opening hooks.
  • The same thing goes: you want anything that is VERBALLY intriguing.

  • Make your verbal hook visual by writing it as text on screen.
  • Give the viewer a reason to wait until the end with a verbal hook.

2. A Reason To Keep Watching

  • There’s an infinite number of ways to do this but I’ll boil it down.
  • Basically the viewer will keep watching if either:
    • It’s really entertaining (ex. a dance video: there’s not really an open loop but the video is very entertaining)
    • There’s an open loop
  • I recommend doing both obviously, but using an open loop is a fantastic device if you are not sitting on absolute gold footage, and it helps extend watch time.
  • Note: often the hook bleeds into the open loop, and that’s good!
  • An open loop is basically some sort of question (can be explicit or implied) that the viewer wants to see resolved.
  • When I say a “question” do not take it super literally… a LOT of things can be an open loop.
  • Once the loop is closed, viewer interest drops significantly!
  • Even if the viewer sees something that answers the question, that can close the loop.
    • The stronger they want to see the loop get closed, the more engaged they will be.

3. A Shareable Moment

  • This last piece is not REQUIRED to make a viral video, but a LOT have them.
  • It increases the shareability (the second most important metric).
  • AND increases the rewatch rate… making our skip rate EVEN better.
  • Basically I put a little crazy moment at the end that makes the viewer go “what was that”, and then they have to rewatch the video again, and it makes them laugh and share it to a friend.

Other Very Important Pro Tips

1Copy copy copy
  • The absolute most guaranteed and most reliable way to make a viral video is to copy what already worked.
  • There are 34 MILLION new videos uploaded to TikTok every single day, and the algorithm pushes the best of the best of the best to the top.
  • It is possible to make completely new stuff and beat the 34 million (I’ve done it) but trust me, a billion views later, I wouldn’t do it again. It is FAR easier to look at the cream of the crop and just copy them.
  • Lucky for you, we have a library of successful videos from other creators (hint: replicate stuff with higher views).
2Cut out dead space
3Make your videos visually simple and easy to understand
4Start in the action
5Trial reels to test
