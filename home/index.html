How accurate is it?

Every number is an estimate. Meal Pic measures portions from the photo, prices each ingredient against nutrition data, and shows the breakdown so you can correct any line. Good light and a photo that fills the frame help a lot.

What does it cost?

Free to download. Meal Pic Pro unlocks unlimited scanning: $29.99 a year (about $2.49 a month) or $9.99 a month, with a 3-day free trial.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions go through Apple: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Meal Pic → Cancel. Deleting the app does not cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends to avoid a charge.

I paid but still see a paywall

Tap Restore Purchases on the paywall and make sure you're signed in with the Apple ID you bought with.

What happens to my photos?

Before your first scan the app asks for consent to send the photo to our AI provider for analysis. "Not Now" sends nothing. Meals back up to your account; you can delete the account and all its data in-app. Details in the Privacy Policy.

Is this medical advice?

No. Meal Pic is a general wellness tool, not a medical device. Consult a doctor or registered dietitian before making significant changes to your diet.
